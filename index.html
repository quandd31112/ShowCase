Design Philosophy
I design gameplay systems that rely on player interpretation and decision-making, such as emotional inference mechanics, risk-reward choices, and logic-driven puzzle solving. My goal is to let players understand the game through interaction, not exposition, using clear feedback, structured progression, and consistent system rules.
