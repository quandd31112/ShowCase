About me

I design systems that make the next player decision clear, meaningful, and worth considering.

Game Designer with 2 years of independent and project-based game research experience, including 1 year focused on mobile games. My work combines systems thinking, player psychology, level design, and rapid experimentation. Puzzle games shaped how I communicate rules through interaction; roguelites taught me to design meaningful trade-offs; and mobile games taught me to respect every second of the player’s attention.

What motivates meTurning a simple gameplay hook into a system with clear rules, meaningful decisions, and room for mastery.
How I workDefine the player decision, prototype the smallest testable version, observe behavior, and iterate from evidence.
Asset noteSome presentation assets were created with AI-assisted tools. All gameplay systems, mechanics, core loops, progression structures, and level-design work attributed to me were designed and iterated by me.
